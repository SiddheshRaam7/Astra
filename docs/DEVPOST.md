# FORK — A little clarity. A better next move.

## Inspiration

Financial apps tell you what happened. At the moment you need to make a decision, that history rarely answers the question that matters: “Can I do this without making next week harder?” FORK bridges that gap.

## What it does

FORK turns a shared financial model into an interactive map. Income flows into essentials, goals, investments, an emergency reserve, and room to enjoy life. Edit a salary or goal and every connected number responds. An explainable safe-to-spend estimate checks both monthly affordability and actual cash timing. A purchase simulator compares a baseline future with an alternative before any records change.

Money Leaks finds recurring payment patterns and asks you to decide which services still matter. Goal Planning shows required savings, deadlines, and the consequences of changing contributions. Financial X-Ray offers reviewed CSV imports and a credential-gated Gemini document adapter. FORK AI answers through deterministic tools and previews planning changes for explicit confirmation.

## How it addresses the FNB challenge

- **Understanding:** animated money flow, categorized history, source-based document review and recurring payment visibility.
- **Confidence:** explainable spending room, protected buffers and emergency reserves, spending pace, and daily forecasts with assumptions.
- **Action:** manual edits and signed, confirmed assistant changes saved through the same financial model.

## Built with

Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn-style primitives, Radix, Lucide, React Flow, Recharts, Motion, Zustand, Zod, Drizzle and PostgreSQL adapters. Server-side adapters support Gemini, ElevenLabs, Auth0 and Plaid Sandbox.

## What we learned

“Affordable this month” and “available today” are different calculations. AI is most helpful when it understands intent, explains deterministic results, and asks for confirmation before saving a change. A clearer answer is better than a larger dashboard.
